# AI Student OS — Product Idea

## Problem

Students have many different responsibilities, but their tasks are usually scattered across multiple apps and platforms.

School tasks and personal development tasks can easily become mixed together.

As a result, students may know they are busy but still not know what they should do next.

## Solution

AI Student OS will organize tasks into three main categories:

1. SCHOOL
2. GROWTH
3. LIFE

The system will eventually analyze:

- deadlines
- priority
- available time
- workload
- task category

and recommend what the student should work on next.

## Target User

University students who need to manage:

- coursework
- exams
- personal learning
- projects
- internships
- daily life

## Long-Term Goal

The system should eventually be able to answer:

"What should I do today?"

and:

"What should I do right now?"
